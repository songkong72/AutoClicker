1Setup

The app needs one permission to tap the game for you. Every role does this the same way.

  1. On the app's home screen, tap Set up on the Allow screen taps card.
  2. Read the notice and agree. The phone's Accessibility settings open.
  3. Find AutoClicker in the list and turn its switch on.
  4. Go back to the app. When you see Screen taps allowed, you are done.
The switch can turn off after a restart or an app update. If buttons stop responding, turn it off and on again in Accessibility.

2Auto-tap

Taps one spot again and again at a steady interval. Anyone can use it without verification.

  1. Tap the Auto-tap mode card to open Auto-tap settings.
  2. Choose the Tap interval (0.1s, 0.2s, 0.5s, 1.0s, or type your own).
  3. Choose Repeat: Endless keeps going, Count stops after a number of taps, Timer stops after a set time.
  4. Tap Apply, then Show AutoClicker.
  5. Drag the target that appears over the game to the spot you want tapped.
  6. Tap ▶ on the bar to start. Tap it again to stop.
Emergency stop · Press a volume key while it is tapping and it stops at once.

4Rally leader

Get a code from your commander, verify, join the room and lead your team.

Once: verify and join a room

  1. LeaderTell your commander your ID. An email or a nickname both work.
  2. CommanderMakes a code and sends you a message. It contains the ID, rally leader code, group and room number.
  3. LeaderCopy the whole message, then tap Verify on the app's home screen. The fields are already filled in, so check them and tap the button.
  4. On the Rally room card, tap Pick a room and choose the room your commander told you.
  5. Enter your in-game character name. It appears on the commander's list.
  6. CommanderAssigns you to a team. Your row then shows a Me badge.
A rally leader code is AC- plus 6 characters and is tied to your ID and group. Enter the ID exactly as given. After verifying, your group is locked to the commander's group.

Before the rally: save the tap position

  1. Tap Open rally panel at the bottom of the app and go to the game.
  2. In the game, go to the screen that shows the rally button.
  3. Drag the target onto the rally button.
  4. At the bottom of the rally panel, open My device ▾ and tap Save position. You are set when you see ✓ Position saved.
  5. Set your team's march time to the value the game shows (−/+ changes 1s, tap the number to type).
Check this · If you see the yellow text "Save the tap position first" or "Set your character name first", tap it. It takes you straight there.

When the rally starts

  • When the commander starts, the big number counts down to your tap.
  • Meanwhile you must be on the screen that shows the rally button. The app only taps the saved spot.
  • At 0 the app taps for you. Afterwards you see "✓ Tapped" and the time.
  • If the panel is in the way, tap – at the top to fold it into a pill. The countdown stays visible.

5Commander

Creates the room, sets up the teams and starts the rally. Makes codes for rally leaders.

Becoming a commander

  1. Get a commander code (AD- plus 8 characters) from your alliance rep.
  2. Tap the key at the top right of the app's home screen to open Commander login.
  3. Enter the code and tap OK. The Rally leader / Commander tabs appear at the top.
  4. From then on, just tap the Commander tab. No code needed.
A commander code works once and expires (24 hours for codes made by a rep). The group is inside the code, so it is set automatically and cannot be changed.

Create a room and set up teams

  1. Tap Pick a room → Create room. Each group can have up to 10 rooms.
  2. Tap Share number to send the room number to your rally leaders.
  3. Tap Open rally panel, then the pencil at the top of the panel to edit.
  4. Tap + Add team to make teams (up to 10 per room).
  5. Tap a team row to expand it, choose who leads it and enter its march time.
  6. Under Room settings, set Rally time (3, 5 or 10 min) and Prep time (1s steps).
  7. Tap Done to close editing. Every change is saved and sent to everyone as you make it.
The list shows 5 teams at a time. Scroll inside the list for the rest. Tap Room settings ▾ to fold it and the list gets that much longer. The folded line still shows the values.

Start and cancel

  • Tap Start rally and every phone starts counting at the same time. One start is enough.
  • If a joining team is unassigned, the app asks: Start without unassigned teams / Start anyway / Cancel.
  • While running, the same button becomes Cancel rally. Phones that have not tapped yet will not tap.
  • After arrival or a cancel it becomes Rally again. No need to enter the settings again.
  • Teams and times cannot be changed while a rally is running.
Mis-tap guard · For 1.5 seconds after a cancel, "Rally again" in the same spot does not respond.

Making codes for rally leaders

  1. Get the rally leader's ID.
  2. On the Rally room card, tap Manage → Issue rally leader code.
  3. Enter the ID and tap Make rally leader code.
  4. Send the message. The receiver copies the whole message and verifies.
Good to know · Rally leader codes never expire and cannot be taken back. To remove someone, take them off their team.

6Waves Commander

Group teams into waves that arrive some time apart. Teams in one wave arrive together, and from Wave 2 on each wave arrives a set time after Wave 1.

  1. Open editing and tap Split waves to group teams in number order, 2, 3 or 5 per wave. Choose All in Wave 1 to remove waves.
  2. To move one team, expand its row and choose Wave 1 to Wave 5.
  3. Use −/+ on a wave's header to set its delay: 10s steps, from 0s to 10 minutes. Tap the value to type.
  4. Tap Start rally only once. Phones in later waves tap that much later.

7Alliance rep

One per alliance. Does everything a commander does, and also adds and removes the alliance's commanders.

Applying to be the rep

  1. Home screen: key at the top right → Commander login → Apply as our alliance rep.
  2. Enter the server number, alliance and your in-game character name. You can add a note (up to 60 characters, e.g. I lead WBI).
  3. Tap Apply. The home screen shows that the request is pending. You can cancel it there.
  4. After approval, open the request window again and it switches to the commander screen.
There is no approval notification. Open the app now and then to check. One request per phone.

Appointing our alliance's commanders

  1. Tap Manage → Our alliance commanders.
  2. Tap + Make commander code, enter a label, make the code and send it.
  3. The receiver becomes a commander by entering the code in Commander login within 24 hours.
  4. To remove someone, tap Delete on the list. That phone goes back to the rally leader screen the next time the app opens.
RuleValue
Commanders per allianceUp to 5, including the rep.
What the rep managesTheir own alliance only. Other alliances are not visible.
Commanders appointing commandersNot possible. Only the rep appoints.
Changing the repSomeone else in the alliance applies, and once approved the rep changes. The former rep becomes a regular commander.

8Group (server · alliance)

Rooms belong to a group. Only people in the same group meet in the same room. It looks like 2000 · WBI on screen.

  • Server number: up to 6 digits. Alliance: up to 8 letters and digits.
  • Alliance is case-sensitive. WBI and wbi are different groups.
  • Room numbers are separate for each group. Another alliance using the same number does not clash.
  • Changing your group takes you out of the room you were in.
RoleHow the group is set
Rally leaderCopy the invite and verify: the group in the invite is used and locked. To move to another alliance, get a new code from that alliance's commander.
Commander, repIt is inside the commander code. It shows a lock and cannot be changed.

9Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Buttons work but the game is not tappedTurn AutoClicker off and on again in Accessibility. See Setup
"Available after you verify with a rally leader code."You have not verified yet. See Rally leader
The code does not matchCheck that the ID is exactly as given and that the alliance's upper and lower case match. Old-style codes are not accepted, so get a new one.
Our room is not in the listCheck that your group (server · alliance) is the same as the commander's. See Group
My name is not on the commander's listCheck that you joined the room and entered a character name. If it is still missing, leave the room and join again.

A little later or earlier than the othersAdjust the Offset under My device in 0.5s steps. − is earlier, + is later.
"Start rally" is dimmedNo team is joining. The red text below tells you why.
